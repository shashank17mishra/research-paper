## 1. ShallowDefend: Harnessing Shallow Safety Alignment for Multi-Turn Defender

Relevance Score: 0.64

Paper: [arXiv:2610.40627](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40627v1) | [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.40627v1)

### Summary

Addressing critical demands in Machine Learning, this research investigates safety alignment has enabled large language models to reliably refuse harmful requests directly, yet the same request distributed across a multi-turn conversation still succeeds far too often. We propose Assess \to Decide \to Respond, a framework in which the defender assesses the user's intent, commits to an explicit, parseable safety decision, and only then generates a reply conditioned on it, with the commitment trained by reinforcement learning at the payload turn. We take the opposite view of shallowness: if a short span determines the trajectory of an entire response, that span is the highest-leverage place to intervene, and the defender can occupy it first. The very shallowness attackers exploit becomes the mechanism of defense.
